Overview

Retail Spaces That
Sell the Brand.

From boutique flagships to multi-location rollouts, we build retail with opening dates in mind and longevity built in.

Retail is theatre. Lighting, materials, and flow either move product or kill momentum — and the difference is rarely accidental. CVC Group has spent two decades delivering retail environments that perform commercially and hold up under daily traffic.

Capabilities

What We Build.

Flagship & Boutique Builds

Premium fit-outs where finish quality, lighting design, and feature millwork carry the brand story.

Multi-Location Rollouts

Repeatable construction systems that maintain brand standards across 5, 25, or 100+ locations on aggressive timelines.

Mall & High-Street Fit-Outs

Landlord coordination, after-hours work windows, and noise/dust control protocols that keep neighbouring tenants happy.

Pop-Up & Seasonal Builds

Short-cycle construction for activations and seasonal concepts — built fast, struck cleanly, no surprises.

Display, Fixture & Millwork

Custom shelving, gondolas, and feature displays produced in-house and installed by the same team that built the box.

Refresh & Renovation

Brand refresh programs delivered in-place, often overnight, with stores reopening the next morning.

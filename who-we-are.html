Who We Are

Two Decades of Building
Spaces That Matter.

CVC Group is a Concord, Ontario-based construction company with 22+ years of experience delivering exceptional retail, hospitality, and commercial environments across Canada.

Our Story

Craftsmanship Backed
By Experience.

For over 22 years, CVC Group has partnered with some of Canada's most recognized brands to design and build the environments where their stories come to life. From boutique retail to high-volume hospitality and complex commercial fit-outs, we've built our reputation one space at a time.

What started as a small team of trades has grown into a full-service construction partner — 265+ skilled tradespeople strong — with the depth to handle every phase from pre-construction planning to final turnover.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way.

2003

CVC Group is Founded

Started in Concord, Ontario with a small, focused crew and an uncompromising approach to quality retail fit-outs for local businesses.

2009

First National Brand Partnership

Began long-term partnerships with multi-location retailers, expanding our capabilities and team depth across Ontario.

2014

Hospitality Division Launched

Added dedicated hospitality expertise — restaurants, cafés, and lounges — bringing the finish quality and speed that opening-driven projects demand.

2019

Commercial Expansion

Scaled into commercial interiors and office build-outs, completing our three-pillar service offering: retail, hospitality, commercial.

2025

189+ Projects Delivered

Continuing to build long-term partnerships with brands across Canada. 265+ skilled tradespeople and counting — still building spaces that matter.
